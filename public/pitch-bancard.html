The Opportunity

Why a bilateral rail?

Traditional cross-border payment corridors involve multiple intermediaries, high fees, and settlement delays. A bilateral IP agreement removes all of that — you hold prefunded balances and settle directly, in real time.

⚡

Real-time settlement

Transfers confirmed and settled instantly between both institutions. No T+1, no T+2 delays.

💸

Zero intermediary fees

Direct bilateral agreement — no correspondent bank fees, no SWIFT charges, no FX markups imposed by third parties.

🔒

HMAC-SHA256 authentication

Every request is signed with a shared secret. Tamper-proof, auditable, and zero trust by design.

📡

Webhook-driven

Your system receives instant push notifications on every transfer event — no polling required.

Integration Flow

How it works

Both institutions run the same open protocol. Connecting takes days, not months.

1

Submit institutional data & documents

Fill out the partner portal with your institution details, license number, regulatory body, and upload required compliance documents (LEI, license, incorporation, etc.)

2

Compliance review & approval

Our compliance team reviews all submitted documents. KYC/AML checks are run. You receive a decision within 2–5 business days.

3

Technical onboarding

Exchange API credentials (key_id + secret). Both sides configure webhook endpoints. Run a test transfer to confirm connectivity.

4

Go live

Prefund a balance on both sides. Start transacting. Full audit trail on every operation from day one.
